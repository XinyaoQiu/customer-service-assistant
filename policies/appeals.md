## Appeals > Eligibility

Copyright, duplicate, and policy-violation decisions can be appealed within 30 days of
the review date. Some decisions are not appealable; the article's status will indicate
which.

## Appeals > How to file

Open the article in the Creator dashboard and select "Request review". Include any
licenses, source links, or context that was not visible to the original reviewer.

## Appeals > Timing

Appeals are answered within five business days. Filing an appeal does not pause other
review processes on the account.
