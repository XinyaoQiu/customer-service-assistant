## Media Licensing > Permitted sources

Creators may use media they produced, media licensed for commercial redistribution, or
public-domain works. A license on file is required for anything else.

## Media Licensing > Wire service material

Photographs from wire services require a license held by the creator. Attribution alone
does not grant a license.

## Media Licensing > Consequences

Articles containing unlicensed media are rejected. Repeated instances may affect
account standing.
