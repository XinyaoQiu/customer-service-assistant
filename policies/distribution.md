## Distribution > What creators can see

The Creator dashboard reports impressions, clicks, and read time for each article,
alongside the account's own historical averages.

## Distribution > What determines reach

Reach is decided by a ranking system that weighs many signals and changes over time.
NewsBreak does not publish the signals or their weights, and support cannot explain why
a specific article performed the way it did.

## Distribution > Practices that tend to help

Publishing on a consistent schedule, covering topics with local relevance, writing
headlines that describe the article accurately, and including original photography.
