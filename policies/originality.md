## Original Content > What qualifies

An article qualifies as original when the reporting, writing, and framing are the
creator's own work. Aggregating facts from other outlets is permitted when the article
adds reporting, local context, or analysis that did not exist in the sources.

## Original Content > What does not qualify

Republishing another outlet's article, lightly rewriting a press release, or
machine-translating existing content does not qualify, regardless of attribution.

## Original Content > Self-syndication

Creators may republish their own work from another platform, provided they hold the
rights. Substantially identical articles posted repeatedly to the same account are
treated as duplicates.
