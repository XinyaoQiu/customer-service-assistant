## Review > Timing

Submitted articles are normally reviewed within 48 hours. Volume during major news
events can extend this.

## Review > Statuses

Draft, pending review, scheduled, published, and rejected. An article in pending review
has not yet been evaluated; no decision has been made about it.

## Review > After publication

Published articles remain subject to review and can be removed if a later check finds a
policy issue.
