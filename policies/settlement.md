## Payout Timing > Standard cycle

Earnings for a calendar month are issued on a T+7 basis: payment is initiated seven
business days after the close of the month in which the earnings were recorded.

## Payout Timing > Holidays

When the payout date falls on a bank holiday, settlement is deferred to the next
business day. No action is required from the creator.

## Payout Timing > Minimum threshold

Balances below $50 roll over to the following month rather than being paid out.
